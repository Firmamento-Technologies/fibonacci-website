## 1. What are cookies and other tracking tools

Cookies are small text files that visited websites send to the user's device (computer, tablet, smartphone), where they are stored and retransmitted to the same websites on the next visit. Alongside HTTP cookies, there are other technically equivalent tracking tools, including browser web storage (local storage, session storage), unique device identifiers, tracking pixels (web beacons), and digital fingerprints (fingerprinting).

For the purposes of this policy, the following distinctions are made:

- Session cookies, which are deleted when the browser is closed, and persistent cookies, which remain stored on the device for a set period;
- First-party cookies, installed directly by the Data Controller of the visited website, and third-party cookies, installed by entities other than the Data Controller;
- Technical cookies, used solely to transmit a communication over an electronic communications network or to the extent strictly necessary for the provider of an information society service explicitly requested by the user; such cookies do not require the data subject's prior consent, pursuant to Article 122 of the Privacy Code;
- Profiling and marketing cookies, aimed at creating user profiles and used to send advertising messages in line with the preferences expressed by the user while browsing the web; such cookies require the data subject's free, specific, informed, granular, and unambiguous consent.

## 4. Profiling, marketing, and non-anonymized statistical cookies

Currently, the Fibonacci showcase website and the Fibonacci application do not use profiling cookies, marketing cookies, or non-anonymized statistical cookies. No tools are present that allow the creation of user behavioral profiles, advertising retargeting, or the sharing of identifiers with third-party platforms.

Should such tools be introduced in the future, the Data Controller will first:

1. Display a first-visit banner compliant with the Garante's Provision of 10 June 2021, including a brief notice and a link to the extended information;
2. Allow a free, granular, and revocable choice, with at least three equivalent options in terms of graphics, typography, and prominence: "Accept all," "Reject all," and "Manage preferences";
3. Avoid using pre-selected checkboxes (no pre-flagging) and not interpret scrolling, closing the banner, or continuing navigation as consent;
4. Ensure the user can revoke consent at any time with the same ease as it was given;
5. Renew the consent request no later than 6 months after the previous expression of will, or if the processing conditions or third-party recipients change significantly, in accordance with paragraph 7 of the Garante's Guidelines;
6. Keep a record of the consent given and its date to demonstrate compliance pursuant to Article 7(1) GDPR;
7. Update this Cookie Policy with a detailed list of the activated profiling and marketing cookies, their purposes, third-party recipients, transfer countries, and adopted safeguards.
