# Literature Search

The **Literature** page queries PubMed directly from the patient record, without opening another browser tab or leaving the visit context.

## How to search

Enter one or more terms and confirm. The search is routed through PubMed (NCBI): we do not maintain our own article archive, nor do we store search history.

Filters for **publication type** narrow down the results:

- **Randomized controlled trials**: experimental evidence.
- **Meta-analyses**: quantitative syntheses of multiple studies.
- **Reviews**: overviews of a topic.
- **Practical guidelines**: operational recommendations from scientific societies.
- **Case reports**: individual cases, useful for rare complications.

Without filters, all results are displayed, ordered as returned by PubMed.

## What you see for each result

Title, authors, journal, year, and, when available from PubMed, the **abstract**. If the abstract is missing, the card explicitly states so instead of leaving a blank space.

When an article is open access, **Open PDF full-text** appears. For paywalled articles, the link is not provided: we do not hold journal subscriptions, nor do we simulate access.

## When the search does not respond

- **«Enter at least one search term»**: the field is empty.
- **Search error**: PubMed did not respond. The service is public and free within a few requests per second. If you are searching too quickly, wait a moment and `Retry`.

## A limitation worth knowing

The search is **in English**, as PubMed indexes in English. Searching for «acido ialuronico» yields far fewer results than «hyaluronic acid». We do not automatically translate terms: an incorrect translation would silently alter results, and in clinical practice, that is worse than no results at all.
