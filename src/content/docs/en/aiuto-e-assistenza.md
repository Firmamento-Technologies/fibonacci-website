# Where to get help: manual, assistant, support

At the bottom of the navigation menu, there are four options to use when something goes wrong. They serve different purposes, and knowing which one to use saves time.

| Option | What it is | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| `AI guided tour` | A walkthrough of the main areas, on-screen | On your first day, or when a new team member joins |
| `Guides and manual` | This manual | "How do I do X" |
| The assistant in the bottom right | Answers by reading the manual | "Where is X", without stopping work |
| `Get support` | Writes to us | Something isn’t working |

## The guided tour

`AI guided tour` takes you through the dashboard, patients, medical records, treated areas, photos, consents, appointments, reminders, drugs, literature, atlas, and audit log in a couple of minutes. It plays automatically, and you can pause, go back, or skip ahead. It starts automatically the first time you log in from the demo; after that, you can relaunch it from this button.

## The manual

`Guides and manual` opens the manual **within the application**: chapter index on the left, with search at the top; text in the center; on wide screens, the headings of the chapter you’re reading on the right. The **←** and **→** keyboard arrows change chapters, and at the bottom of each chapter, there are links to the previous and next ones.

Each chapter has its own address (`/manuale/<chapter>`), so you can send a colleague a link to a specific chapter instead of the entire manual.

⚠️ **The manual is not published on the website.** Until August 12, 2026, the guides were public pages on `fibonaccimedica.it`; they were removed to avoid showing competitors everything the product does. They can only be read from here, with an active session, and the text isn’t included in the program downloaded by the browser: it comes from the server after access is verified.

⚠️ **The manual updates with releases, not automatically.** If a screen has changed and the guide still describes the old version, it’s not your mistake: report it.

## The in-app assistant

The box in the bottom right (`Ask the AI`) answers questions about the product’s functions **by reading these same guides**, and it knows which screen you’re writing from. Three things to know:

- **If the answer isn’t in the guides, it says so** instead of making something up, and directs you to contact support. This is intentional: an assistant that fills gaps is worse than one that stays silent.
- **It doesn’t replace clinical judgment**, and it states this under every answer. For dosages, it refers to the `Drugs` section.
- **`Clear` resets the conversation.**

## Contacting support

`Get support` opens a pre-written message to our inbox. Before sending, you see exactly what’s included:

- **What you write** in `What’s happening?`;
- **The `Attached technical report`**, which is the last error logged in this browser session, shown in full before sending. If there are no errors, the window states: *"No errors logged in this session: only what you write will be sent"*.

⛔ **Do not include patient data here**, and the warning is inside the field. The conversation with the assistant **is not attached**, intentionally: it may contain names you’ve searched.

`Open the message` sends the text to your email program: sending it remains your action.

⚠️ If the `Get support` option doesn’t appear, the support email isn’t configured for this installation. A button that opens an email to nowhere is worse than no button, so in that case, it isn’t displayed.

## Installing the app on a tablet or phone

`Install the app` appears when the browser is ready to install it: it adds Fibonacci to the home screen, full-screen, without the address bar. It’s useful for the tablet used for questionnaires and the camera in the clinic.

Two intentional behaviors:

- **The button doesn’t disappear** if the installation fails: it changes to `Retry installation`, and the page reloads to get a new prompt from the browser;
- **Its absence is information, not a fault**: some browsers don’t offer installation, and on iPhone, you use "Add to Home Screen" from Safari’s share menu.
