# The Dashboard: What to Check in the Morning

> ⚠️ **Verified by opening the screen on 2026-08-10.** The items below are exactly what you see; if something doesn’t match, it’s a guide defect: report it.

The dashboard is the page you land on after logging in. It’s not a statistics dashboard: it’s designed to answer just one question: *what’s waiting for me today?*

## How It’s Structured

From top to bottom:

1. **Four indicators**: *Patients in record*, *Treatments performed*, *Active consents*, *Emails delivered*. Below each, it specifies **the period they refer to**: patients are the practice’s total and don’t follow the selector, while the other three do.
2. **Today’s appointments.** In chronological order, in chronological order. This is the real reason you open a dashboard in the morning, which is why it’s placed above everything except the numbers.
3. **To-do.** Open reminders and callbacks: patients the system flagged for follow-up and that no one has closed yet.

## The Period Selector

The four options are **Today · 7 days · 30 days · Always**.

⚠️ **Doesn’t control the entire page**: it affects the three indicators that state “in the selected period,” while *Patients in record* is the practice’s total, *today’s appointments* always show today (changing them based on a filter wouldn’t make sense), and the *to-do* items remain open, regardless of when they were created.

If a number doesn’t change when adjusting the period, it’s not a defect: below it, it states it’s “independent of the period.”

## What You WON’T Find Here

- **Revenue and collections.** The dashboard is clinical, not administrative.
- **Technical resource names.** Until August 2026, subtitles were written in implementation jargon; today, every item is in clinical language. If you find one you wouldn’t say out loud to a colleague, report it: it’s a defect.

## If the Page Is Empty

A newly opened practice sees zeros everywhere, and that’s correct. The day remains empty until there’s an appointment scheduled for today: create one from **`Appointments`**.
